# 01 — Product Overview

## What Awaaz is

Awaaz is **one product with two superpowers**:

1. **Voice Changer Studio** — the main, everyday app. Click a preset, sound like someone else live in your browser (gender morph, robot, radio, celebrity-inspired archetypes, etc.).
2. **Lie Detector Arena** — a party-game subset built on the same audio engine. Players wear voice masks, one sits in the "hot seat," and a live polygraph reads vocal stress to catch the liar.

Plus **solo coaching tools** (public-speaking, interview practice) that reuse the stress engine without multiplayer.

## Core tension (the product's soul)

> The **mask** hides *who* you are. The **polygraph** reads *how you feel*. Disguise your voice — not your nerves.

The voice changer gives people anonymity (which makes them comfortable enough to lie convincingly). The stress engine peeks behind the mask (which creates the drama). Both share one audio graph: morph chain for transmission, raw-mic analysis for truth.

## Target users

- **Party / game nights** — 3–6 friends over a link, no installs.
- **Content creators** — record morphed monologues, prank-style audio, character bits.
- **Speakers / interviewees** — solo coaching on pauses, fillers, composure.
- **Tinkerers** — people who enjoy real-time audio effects and DSP knobs.

## Platforms

- Desktop browsers first (Chrome, Edge, Firefox, Safari).
- Mobile works but is secondary (portrait HUD layout provided).
- No accounts, no downloads, no tracking in v1.

## Honest framing (must appear in-product)

- The polygraph measures **vocal arousal** (autonomic nervous system effects on the voice), **not deception itself**. Real polygraphs don't detect lies either — they detect stress.
- The verdict engine is deliberately imperfect and game-balanced; post-game stats show **detector accuracy** so the machine's fallibility is part of the fun.
- A short in-lobby disclaimer: *"For entertainment. Not a scientific lie detector."*
- Vocal-health views carry: *"Not a medical device."*

## Success looks like (v1)

- A 3-browser playthrough completes without dead-ends.
- Male↔female morph is perceptible in a blind A/B test at ±5 semitones.
- Calm truth-tellers score visibly lower than panicked players in playtests (biases, not certainties).
- Total infra cost remains **$0.00/month**.
